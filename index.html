From the wok

The kind of Chinese dinner you carry home in a warm paper bag.

Golden Choice has been feeding the families on Sharpley Road and the streets around it — a takeaway you ring or walk to, pick up your bag, eat at home.

The kitchen does what a good neighbourhood Chinese is supposed to do. Hot from the pan, generous on the plate, in your hands inside the half-hour. No fuss, no theatre, no twenty-page menu you have to scroll through.

Customers describe the food as delicious and authentic, with generous portions and fast delivery.
